Activities

The Knights organise some weekly and monthly activities:

  • Weekly Board Game Evenings – Every Tuesday evening, you can join us in the living room to play board games. We recommend showing up at least 5 to 10 minutes early to find out about upcoming activities, socialise and find a group to play with.

  • Wednesday activities – Every Wednesday we (try to) organise a special activity. This includes:

    • Role-playing one-shots every first Wednesday of the month
    • Various trading card activities
    • Role-playing workshops
    • Special one-off activities

  • Sword Fighting Training – Every two weeks, we host a training session where you can practise sword fighting — or, more accurately, hit each other with foam swords. We recommend showing up a bit earlier.

  • Messing Schaar: Arts & Crafts – Once a month, members gather for an arts & crafts day, where you can get help finding the right materials to buy, and work on cutting and sewing them into your LARP costume. You can also work on other fun crafting projects!

  • Members weekend – Twice a year, we go away for a themed weekend of Knights fun. During the weekend we organise a LARP, multiple role playing one-shots, other unique activities, and plenty of time to play your favourite boardgames!

  • Special Events – We also run special events throughout the year, such as:

    • Find your Roleplaying group: An event where you can find a group to play a role-playing campaign like DnD with. If you would like to run a campaign and pitch it at this event, please contact the roleplaying committee using the email address listed in Squire.
    • Crazy cloak construction: A full day dedicated to creating your own cloak, with no prior experience required.
    • Mini-painting workshop
